Our Mission

Not "you're wrong" — but "there might be another perspective"

Algorithms are designed to keep you watching — feeding you more of what you already believe. Over time, this creates echo chambers that narrow your worldview and deepen polarization.

EchoBreaker is built on a simple idea: every argument deserves to meet its strongest counterpart. We don't judge what you watch. We help you see the full picture.

Echo Chamber Intensity

Analysis generated to promote critical thinking, intellectual humility, and perspective diversity. Counter-perspectives represent possible opposing viewpoints, not established facts. Always verify claims through independent research.

Built with Responsible AI Principles

Transparency, privacy, and human agency at every layer.

Privacy-First Design

Zero data stored. No cookies, no tracking, no user accounts. Captions processed in-memory and discarded immediately.

Balanced, Not Biased

Three dimensions — ethical, empirical, logical — ensure no single mode of reasoning dominates.

Human Agency First

Counter-perspectives are invitations, not corrections. You decide what to think — we ensure you've seen the other side.
